Deployment template provenance
============================

The Cosmos, Sales OS, and Marketing OS interfaces were supplied by the user
for adaptation as Baalda templates. Original interfaces: Ben AI.

Sources:
- https://benai-cosmos.vercel.app/
- https://cockpit-nebula-h7q2k.vercel.app/
- https://marketing-os-control.vercel.app/#/intel

Retrieved 2026-10-13. Upstream ownership and attribution are retained; the
deployments did not include a separate source license. This notice does not
relicense their interfaces. Baalda-authored adaptations use Apache-2.0.

All original business records, contact details, portraits, video thumbnails,
service action handlers, and account-specific links have been removed. The
bundled examples are fictional, with session-only demo interactions.
